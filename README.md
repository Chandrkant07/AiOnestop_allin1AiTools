# AiOnestop – All-in-One AI Tools 🚀🤖

A powerful, browser-based productivity platform offering a suite of AI-powered tools including a Daily Planner, Pomodoro Timer, Resume Builder, Caption/Bio Generator, and more — all in one place.

🌐 **Live Site:** [AiOnestop on Netlify](https://zingy-chimera-c9e503.netlify.app)

---

## 🔧 Features

- ✅ **Daily Planner** – Organize your tasks with priority and time slots  
- ⏱️ **Pomodoro Timer** – Stay focused using the proven 25/5 technique  
- 📝 **Resume Builder (AI)** – Generate job-tailored resumes with smart suggestions  
- 🧠 **Caption & Bio Generator** – Create engaging LinkedIn & Instagram content instantly  
- 🎯 **User-Friendly UI** – Clean, mobile-responsive design  
- ☁️ **Deployed on Netlify** – Fast, reliable, and free hosting
